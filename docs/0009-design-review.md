## The cheap controls may already remove the entire primary mechanism

In the four-cluster condition, all source points belonging to one wrong cluster
are within 0.8 pixels of one another. The common 1-pixel greedy source exclusion
therefore retains **at most one wrong pair per cluster**, hence at most four
cluster-origin wrong pairs total. This is a deterministic bound even though the
actual true-pair retention and Gaussian residuals remain random. It removes the
original count inflation almost by construction. Eight or sixteen spatially
spread true pairs will usually be a much stronger explanation, though no
unconditional success bound follows: Gaussian support is not guaranteed and
near-location conflicts can also remove true pairs. Half of the primary family
is therefore principally a dedup sanity/control condition, not convincing
territory for an exact-tail gain.

The twelve-cluster condition also offers an easy cue to the cell control: its
wrong endpoints occupy a small region of the 512-square image, while true pairs
are spread over most of it. The fixed marginal cell weights tend to penalize that
localized accidental support. Excess N-S/n further subtracts the exact graph's
expected accidental count, rather than relying on an imperfect cell surrogate.
The exact tail has to add useful information beyond **both** advantages. The
coarse grid can merge clusters or split a cluster at a boundary, but that does
not establish eight recoverable cases for higher-order counting. Global shifts
vary phase; they do not erase the systematic spatial distinction between the
true and wrong marginals.

Thus a near-perfect strongest control is plausible before any outcome. It is not
proven: finite assignments, noise, retention and graph structure can change the
ranking, and no actual seeds were evaluated in this review. If the best control
exceeds 56/64, the predeclared +8 target is arithmetically unreachable. Report
insufficient headroom and stop this candidate. Lowering the target, changing
cluster radius, hiding the four-cluster half, weakening the grid control or
removing shared dedup would manufacture an easier comparison after inspection.

## The artificial null is close to old a-contrario reasoning

The cached primary-source audit already establishes spatial accident correction,
random coupling and hypothesis significance as prior art. The distinguishable
ingredient under consideration is exact finite fixed-endpoint assignment
counting, not the random-coupling story or classical rook arithmetic. Its vision
novelty is unresolved. This generator intentionally supplies nonuniform random
couplings, so success would establish a useful statistic only under this very
specific artificial stressor. It would not validate descriptor-selected matches,
justify a general geometry method, or establish a new research contribution.

There is an additional null caveat even **within this synthetic screen**. Greedy
pairwise dedup accepts a pair using both its assigned source and target conflicts.
Changing the target permutation can therefore change the retained endpoints and
representatives. Conditional on retention, the assignment is not shown to remain
uniform over all n! retained-endpoint permutations. True pairs are also mixed
into every case. The generator's initial wrong-pair randomization must not be
promoted into a calibrated full-sample or selected-H p-value claim. This does not
invalidate comparing the score as a heuristic using observed transfer accuracy.

## The current safeguards expose, rather than solve, method weaknesses

Compact true support can be penalized relative to a few isolated false pairs;
exact tails do not universally repair that task mismatch. The existing counting
counterexamples already establish that limitation without proving which of these
fixed synthetic cases will realize it. Compact true clouds can also create
connected non-bicliques with a side larger than the exact solver's cap of 12.
That is an important practical refusal, not a legitimate reason to ignore that
candidate or choose a supported subset. Zero such failures is a strict but clear
continuation requirement for this bounded implementation. The 900-second and
per-case caps may reject it on tractability before any claimed quality benefit.

The fixed translation pool avoids proposal self-fitting and GT tie bias, but
contains exact truth and a deliberately planted false mode. Its outcome says
nothing about finding a useful model among realistically generated candidates,
scoring rotations/projective transforms, or fitting a model from limited support.
Missing ORSA/GMS/MAGSAC comparisons are acceptable only for this early stop/go
screen; they cannot be waived for a future practical claim.
