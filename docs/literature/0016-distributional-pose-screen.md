# 0016 — distributional relative-pose opportunity screen

Source-only review, 2026-10-19. No experiment admission, code, new asset, image/HDF5 read, estimator call, or claim of improvement accompanies this note. It follows the failed 0015 control; it does not reopen that control or change its gates.

**Decision:** reject “keep several matches / fit a mixture / integrate matching probability along epipolar lines” as a novelty claim. Direct calibrated-camera precedents already contain both maximization and marginalization over ambiguous correspondences. A small information-content diagnostic is defensible; a new pose solver is not yet justified. In particular, a lower photometric minimum need not mean a better correspondence, and retaining the rest of a biased cost surface need not repair the bias.

## Closest primary precedents and reading status

### Direct camera geometry: the decisive older precedent

**Domke and Aloimonos, _A Probabilistic Notion of Correspondence and the Epipolar Constraint_, 3DPVT 2006.** Author-hosted PDF full text inspected, especially §§2, 4, 6.2; not merely its abstract. With source point `s`, discrete target candidates `q`, correspondence mass `rho_s(q)`, and normalized epipolar line `l(E,s)`, their Eq. 7 is

\[
\rho(E)\propto\prod_s\left[\max_q\rho_s(q)\exp\{-\big(q^\top l(E,s)\big)^2\}+\alpha\right].
\]

Section 6.2, Eqs. 14–20, also derives the **sum** version by integrating Gaussian-smoothed correspondence density over the epipolar line. Thus neither a max-mixture nor epipolar probability integration is an unexplored principle. Their image evidence uses Gabor phase; geometry is calibrated essential-matrix estimation, with random initialization and local optimization. This is a direct predecessor, unlike object-pose methods requiring a CAD model. The paper discusses distribution-construction cost and motion-search cost separately. Its old experiments are not evidence that our local appearance costs will help modern DKM/PoseLib. [Author PDF](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~domke/papers/20063dpvt.pdf), [author publication/venue listing](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~jdomke/pubs.html).

The companion **_A Probabilistic Notion of Camera Geometry: Calibrated vs. Uncalibrated_, PCV 2006**, full text §§2–3 inspected, makes the essential/fundamental-matrix distinction explicit: Eqs. 8–12 combine distribution-based scoring with `F=K^{-T}[t]_x R K^{-1}`. Unknown or uncertain calibration does not create an otherwise missing generic probabilistic formulation. [Author PDF](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~jdomke/papers/2006pcv.pdf).

**Schlesinger, Flach and Shekhovtsov, _A Higher Order MRF-Model for Stereo-Reconstruction_, DAGM 2004.** Full PDF §§2–3 inspected. Eq. 1 is a joint image/correspondence model with appearance, geometric, and surface-prior energies. Its EM procedure estimates the fundamental matrix from posterior correspondence marginals. It assumes a continuous, largely binocular-visible surface and uses Gibbs sampling. This additionally rules out generic “jointly alternate geometry and soft matching” or “add a spatial model” as sufficient novelty. The printed extracted optimization signs are not used here as implementation specifications. [Author PDF](https://wwwpub.zih.tu-dresden.de/~ds24/stereo/HX6X7ACXF0P59N90.pdf).

### Recent dense matching: a concrete compression point, not a new theorem

**Edstedt et al., _RoMa: Robust Dense Feature Matching_, CVPR 2024.** Full arXiv HTML §§3.1–3.4 inspected. Eq. 8 models coarse conditional correspondence as `sum_k pi_k(x_A) B_{m_k}`. Eq. 9 selects the highest-probability anchor and averages its local four-neighbor neighborhood; subsequent refinement receives one deterministic warp. This identifies an actual point at which separated alternatives can be discarded. It does not establish that retaining those alternatives improves pose or that top-K continuation is novel. Our cached DKM coordinates do not contain these RoMa logits. [Full text, v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.15404v2), [CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Edstedt_RoMa_Robust_Dense_Feature_Matching_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf).

**Edstedt et al., _RoMa v2: Harder Better Faster Denser Feature Matching_, ECCV 2026 camera-ready, arXiv v3 dated 2026-07-06.** Current v3 full HTML §§3.2–3.3, 4.8 and Appendix E inspected, superseding an initial v1 read. Its coarse architecture uses a multi-view transformer and regression heads; it is not simply the original RoMa anchor decoder. It predicts full positive-definite 2×2 pixel precision and tests covariance-aware post-refinement and RANSAC scoring. Consequently point-plus-isotropic-noise is insufficient as a future strong comparison. Appendix E uses forward/backward uncertainty, but does not retain separated target modes in the pose residual. Its rendered Eq. 20 has notation issues (including an unsquared numerator), so an eventual reproduction needs official-code verification, not literal transcription. [Current full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.15706v3), [version/venue metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15706), [official code](https://github.com/Parskatt/RoMaV2).

**Barroso-Laguna et al., _Matching 2D Images in 3D: Metric Relative Pose from Metric Correspondences_ (MicKey), CVPR 2024.** Primary-PDF indexed method text, §3.1.1/Eqs. 1–3, inspected; full-PDF opening failed, so this is **section-level indexed full text**, not a complete-paper read. Its correspondence probability combines both keypoint probabilities and reciprocal descriptor-softmax probabilities:

\[
P(y_{ij})=P_I(i)P(j\mid i)P_{I'}(j)P(i\mid j).
\]

It learns metric 3D keypoints and distributions that guide differentiable RANSAC/Kabsch. This is a recent relative-camera-pose precedent for distribution-driven hypothesis generation and joint training. It is not the same input setting as unknown-depth 2D–2D essential estimation. [CVPR primary PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Barroso-Laguna_Matching_2D_Images_in_3D_Metric_Relative_Pose_from_Metric_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf).

### Adjacent full-distribution pose methods: useful precedents, different inputs

**Germain, Lepetit and Bourmaud, _Neural Reprojection Error_, CVPR 2021.** Primary-PDF indexed §§4–5 inspected; complete PDF opening failed. Its loss maps encode negative correspondence log-probability. Eq. 5 evaluates the map at each pose-projected known 3D point, with an outlier cap; later equations describe truncation/interpolation. Coarse-to-fine optimization and sparse loss-map handling already address computation. It rules out “pose consumes the full matching map instead of a point” as a general contribution. Its **2D–3D absolute-pose** setting supplies known 3D structure absent from our input. [CVPR primary PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Germain_Neural_Reprojection_Error_Merging_Feature_Learning_and_Camera_Pose_Estimation_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf).

**Haugaard and Iversen, _Multi-view object pose estimation from correspondence distributions and epipolar geometry_, arXiv 2022.** Full HTML method inspected. Eq. 2 factors a triplet distribution into `p(u1) p(c|u1) p(u2|u1,c)`; Eq. 8 samples the second image along its epipolar line; Eq. 10 scores poses with correspondence log-probability. It resolves ambiguity using full distributions and geometry, but assumes known object surface and multi-view camera geometry. That is not an unknown relative-camera-pose baseline. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2210.00924), [metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.00924).

**Che et al., _COG: Confidence-aware Optimal Geometric Correspondence for Unsupervised Single-reference Novel Object Pose Estimation_, CVPR 2026.** Full HTML §§3.1–3.2.3 inspected. It combines appearance/geometric affinities, confidence-derived marginals, Sinkhorn transport and pose estimation. Its row-normalized transport produces soft target coordinates `M Q` followed by weighted rigid alignment. Thus “soft matching + transport + geometric confidence” is also established, while taking a barycenter is distinct from retaining separated modes. Its RGB-D/object input makes it an adjacent precedent rather than our same-input baseline. [Full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.00493v1), [CVPR primary PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/papers/Che_COG_Confidence-aware_Optimal_Geometric_Correspondence_for_Unsupervised_Single-reference_Novel_Object_CVPR_2026_paper.pdf).

**Abstract-only screening, not used to establish equations or a novelty gap:** HD3 (CVPR 2019, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.06264)) already decomposes match densities across scales; Parameterized Cost Volume (ICCV 2023, [CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Zeng_Parameterized_Cost_Volume_for_Stereo_Matching_ICCV_2023_paper.html)) uses multiple Gaussian components for stereo. Known-stereo geometry and camera-pose estimation are different tasks. Search absence is not evidence of an unoccupied niche.

## What could be incremental, conditional on evidence

The potentially useful question is **which fine-resolution alternatives must survive compression for geometric pose decisions, under a fixed measurement and optimization budget?** This is narrower than representing uncertainty. For example, mass-dominant modes and modes that distinguish two plausible poses need not coincide. A geometry-sensitive allocation could spend expensive refinement on the latter. That is presently a hypothesis, not a demonstrated new algorithm: active refinement, adaptive discretization, and decision-aware approximation require their own prior-art screen if evidence warrants proceeding.

For Q queries, K alternatives and H pose hypotheses, an explicit discrete compatibility sum costs roughly O(QKH) and stores O(QK) values before additional image features. Reducing K can destroy a small but decisive separated mode; increasing K can make biased appearance support more influential and multiply runtime. Using multiple candidates as independent RANSAC votes also spuriously multiplies each source query's evidence. Per-query normalization/one latent correspondence, outlier mass, source uncertainty, and dependence between overlapping patches cannot be ignored. No general Bayes-optimal interpretation follows from exponentiating an arbitrary SSD map.

The present cache contains points, not the original network's candidate distribution. A local image cost map would be **new classical evidence**, not recovery of discarded DKM probabilities. A four-pixel search can address fine precision, but cannot test global repeated-structure ambiguity. Since the single-mode appearance control failed, the first question is whether its surrounding cost surface contains useful information at all; it is not whether a more elaborate solver can optimize that surface.

## Minimum decisive diagnostic proposed, not admitted

Use only the existing 16 permanent-development pairs and acquired images. Freeze one deterministic cohort before any new image computation: at most the first 64 originally selected 0015 queries per pair, in the original selection order, **without conditioning on fit success, loss decrease, GT error, or correction direction**. Preserve initialization/border failures and all 16 pair denominators.

For an initialized query, hold the original local affine initialization A0 fixed and evaluate a single target-translation grid at 0.5-pixel spacing inside the existing four-pixel disk. Keep the existing 31×31 support, grayscale definition and half-pixel conversion. At each candidate, profile only bounded gain and bias by constrained linear least squares; there is no iterative affine fit, new matcher, geometry-guided search, or repeated optimization. Invalid support remains invalid. This is a different, explicitly restricted measurement from 0015, not a rerun of its optimizer. There are at most 1024 queries ×289 grid locations ×961 samples before disk removal; batching avoids materializing the full image-sample tensor. A protocol would retain the existing CPU/memory ceiling and an unconditional 30-minute cutoff.

Seal the resulting costs and identities before a separate evaluator uses already-authorized development GT. The **primary question is image-rank enrichment**, not a pose improvement claim:

1. Compare the original target, the single lowest-cost candidate, and four image-selected separated minima. Fix deterministic tie-breaking and a one-pixel nonmaximum-suppression radius prospectively; use fewer than four if fewer valid minima exist, with explicit coverage.
2. Compare with the **same number of coordinate-only candidates**, selected from the same valid grid by a fixed seed independent of images and GT. Use the same original-target inclusion rule in both sets. Otherwise an oracle will appear to improve simply because more points can intersect an epipolar line.
3. For diagnostic evaluation only, report the best GT pixel-Sampson compatibility in each candidate set, the image rank of geometry-compatible candidates, the off-mode rescue fraction, and paired medians for every pair. This is an oracle lower bound on candidate-set epipolar error, **not true target-coordinate error, a usable estimator, or pose AUC**. The evaluator must never feed its choices back into candidate generation.

The falsifiable prediction is that image-ranked alternatives contain geometrically useful off-mode locations more often than both the single minimum and the same-budget coordinate-only control. Kill the direction if this enrichment is absent, concentrated in a few selected pairs, or caused only by increased candidate count. A concrete numerical admission threshold must be frozen in a separate protocol before this diagnostic; this note does not retroactively authorize one. Do not search new radii, patch sizes, temperatures, representations or success-only subsets after a failure.

If enrichment is substantial, a subsequent **same-input, same-proposal, same-budget** pose test would compare point scoring, a single-Gaussian approximation, fixed top-K, the full discrete map and the 2006 max/sum objectives. Only then could adaptive representation allocation be evaluated. Full pipeline timing must include map extraction. PoseLib and the official DMS settings remain required strong pose comparisons; modern learned covariance is required if a newer matcher is introduced. Neither a favorable oracle result nor a win over the failed 0015 control would establish novelty, held-out generalization, or a CVPR-level result.
