# Fresh-topic review A: correlated-noise denoising

2026-10-18. **Decision: no new paper direction selected. Do not use known-covariance decorrelation as the core innovation.** Real-camera denoising fits the resource budget, but the proposed principle has strong prior art and the specific center-residual variant has a serious information-loss counterexample. This is a bounded literature/metadata review, not an experiment. No image, label or weight bodies, model inference, cloud execution, paid service, or repository changes.

**R2R formula:** for `y=x+n`, Gaussian `n` with covariance `Σ_x`, independently sample standard Gaussian `z` and form `y₁=y+Az`, `y₂=y−Bz`, choosing `ABᵀ=Σ_x`. Their noise cross-covariance is `Σ_x−ABᵀ=0`; conditional joint Gaussianity supplies independence, and the noisy-target training risk equals the corresponding clean-target risk plus a constant. Thus general-covariance orthogonal self-supervision is already known. This is stronger prior art than merely unknown scalar-noise calibration.

## Exact boundary of the parent’s center-residual idea

The proposal `z_j=y_j−(C_j0/C_00)y_0` has a different construction from R2R and adds no fresh random noise. Its independence argument is nevertheless ordinary Gaussian residualization, not by itself a new theorem.

Our derivation: with **fixed known C**, additive jointly Gaussian noise independent of the clean image, and a predictor restricted to these residuals, `n_j−(C_j0/C_00)n_0` is independent of `n_0`. Consequently `E[(f(Z)−y₀)²]=E[(f(Z)−x₀)²]+C_00`. This valid restricted-input risk identity does not imply full signal recovery. At perfect positive correlation, `C_j0/C_00=1`; on a flat clean patch, the clean DC component cancels. Distinct constant intensities produce identical residual-input distributions. Near that limit the task is ill-conditioned. Feeding the unprojected center back silently would invalidate the stated restriction.

Estimating C from the same noisy neighborhood can reintroduce dependence on the target noise. Cross-fitting limits reuse but is not a general proof for signal-dependent noise; zero covariance alone does not imply independence outside the Gaussian case. Equal-moment two-filter construction and center residualization are different proposals, not one frozen method.

**If screened later:** use fixed synthetic known-covariance flat levels, ramps and repeated textures first. Compare standard blind-spot, projected inputs, and matched-covariance R2R. Real-data controls require noise-estimated BM3D and ZS-N2N, followed by a reproducible contemporary correlated-noise baseline. Gains over only a knowingly violated white-noise baseline are insufficient. No runtime or improvement has been measured.

Official-host HEAD observations: `ValidationNoisyBlocksSrgb.mat` **229,724,517 B** and `ValidationGtBlocksSrgb.mat` **232,055,617 B**, total **461,780,134 B**. README describes 512² crops and 40 source scenes, while crop filenames yield **30 camera/exposure/scene prefixes** after removing crop index. Neither number certifies independent units: group crops by capture and resolve cross-camera scene reuse first. Mean references are multi-capture estimates, not noiseless truth. The acquisition source differs from SIDD; actual content overlap remains unverified.

Combined advertised image payload: **466,468,743 B**, fitting reported remaining **3,238,351,458 B** before metadata/software overhead. Data availability is promising; it does not rescue weak novelty.

## One unresolved narrow question, explicitly not selected

Can a **noisy-only, cross-fitted covariance estimator provide a useful bound on excess self-supervised-risk bias when its covariance estimate is wrong**, so training abstains before absorbing structured noise? The required output would be a calibrated risk-bias bound/decision, not another point estimate of noise variance or pair orthogonality. For arbitrary inputs Z, the noisy-versus-clean risk difference contains `−2E[(f(Z)−x₀)n₀]`; the difficult part is controlling this leakage term with estimated covariance and an adaptive predictor.

This must distinguish itself from generalized Stein risk estimation, plug-in/Monte-Carlo risk estimators, covariance uncertainty bounds and existing noise calibration. Those closest literatures have **not** been audited in this bounded review. Single-image texture/noise ambiguity may make the desired guarantee impossible without explicit assumptions. Therefore this is a question for a future novelty/identifiability kill check, **not an approved innovation or a recommendation to begin training**. A useful first result could be a concrete impossibility counterexample rather than a claimed improvement.
