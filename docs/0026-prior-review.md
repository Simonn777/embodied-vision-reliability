# Nonminimal affine-depth calibration: targeted prior-art addendum

Checked 2026-10-19 by bounded web searches and primary paper/code reading only. No scientific data, model, optimizer or new experiment was accessed. This is not an exhaustive novelty proof.

**Decision:** the broad ideas “nonminimal affine-depth calibration,” “eliminate pose with distances,” and “use a convex relaxation after nuisance elimination” are already covered. I did not locate a primary source implementing the exact combination of fixed product-weight all-pair quartic compression to a 6-by-6 H and the two small cones proposed here. That narrow difference remains a candidate implementation/optimization contribution, not established novelty or sufficient reason for substantial implementation.

## Closest confirmed overlaps

1. **MADPose, CVPR 2025.** Sections 4.1–4.2 already use distance preservation to eliminate rigid pose, recover pose from corrected points, and optimize affine corrections with more than the minimal samples. Equation (9) jointly minimizes depth-induced reprojection and Sampson terms on inlier sets. The official `NonMinimalSolver` and `LeastSquares` invoke `HybridPoseOptimizer` with these terms and a minimum-depth option. Thus a comparison only against its three-point algebraic generator misses its existing nonminimal estimator. The inspected objective is not the complete-pair squared distance-discrepancy quartic. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.05446v3), [official source, lines 165–187 and 233–257](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MarkYu98/madpose/main/src/hybrid_pose_estimator.cpp). The source link is the live main branch read on this date, not a new frozen implementation dependency.

2. **RePoseD / Fixing the Scale and Shift in Monocular Depth for Camera Pose Estimation, 2025.** Section 3.1, equations (8)–(10) and their subsequent pair differences/norm equalities remove translation and rotation from affine-depth equations. Squared scale is already a polynomial lift. This directly prevents claiming the underlying distance elimination or affine depth model as new. The inspected formulation constructs algebraic small-sample solvers; it does not establish our particular all-pair noisy objective or its two-cone relaxation. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.07742v1).

3. **PACE*, RSS 2021: Optimal Pose and Shape Estimation for Category-level 3D Object Perception.** This is a stronger conceptual comparator than generic TEASER. Equation (3) is a weighted nonminimal pose/linear-shape objective; Section IV eliminates translation, solves shape coefficients given rotation, and constructs a 10-variable rotation QCQP and SDP, Eq. (21). Accordingly, nonminimal nuisance elimination followed by a small certifiable convex program is established. Its observed 3D point set is fixed and its other side is a CAD-basis shape model with coefficient constraints/regularization. It does not directly provide bilateral affine monocular-depth calibration or the proposed pair-distance loss. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.08383v2).

4. **NoPe-NeRF, CVPR 2023.** Section 3 optimizes per-image depth scales/shifts together with camera poses; corrected point-cloud alignment uses Chamfer distance, Eq. (8), plus rendering constraints. This is prior nonminimal affine-depth/pose calibration beyond minimal solvers, but it is a joint learned-scene optimization, not a fixed-correspondence moment reduction or convex calibration bound. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.07388v1).

5. **C2P, CVPR 2024.** It formulates nonminimal relative pose as a QCQP/SDP with geometric constraints that incorporate pose disambiguation. It is a relevant standard for what a certifiable nonminimal estimator must establish. Its measurements/objective are epipolar correspondences, not two affine monocular-depth arrays. It does not justify calling a floating-point dual diagnostic a certificate. [Primary paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/papers/Tirado-Garin_From_Correspondences_to_Pose_Non-minimal_Certifiably_Optimal_Relative_Pose_without_CVPR_2024_paper.pdf).

6. **LDVP-SAC, October 2026 preprint.** A targeted “monocular depth + variable projection” search found this current paper. Equations (9), (13), (14) eliminate free ray depths and profile horizontal camera position, leaving rotation. Its observation model is perspective-to-georeferenced orthographic geometry; monocular depth informs structural solver selection. It is not affine-depth calibration of two perspective images. [Primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.14945v1). It rules out a broad variable-projection novelty claim, not this exact objective.

## Algebraic distinction and investment limit

Our inference from the models, rather than a claim made by PACE: with
`R(p_i+b r_i)+t-(a p'_i+c r'_i)`, nuisance coefficients are linear for fixed R, but their normal matrix contains rotation-dependent cross terms such as `(R r_i)^T p'_i`. Thus PACE's one-sided shape elimination does not automatically turn this bilateral problem into its same quadratic rotation program. Conversely, the same second moments already permit O(1)-in-N proper-Procrustes profiling of `(b,a,c)`, as derived in the separate math review. Fast cached evaluation alone is therefore a weak distinguishing claim.

The smallest defensible remaining question is whether this particular fixed-weight relaxation produces useful physical initializations or bounds, at lower total cost or better downstream outcomes than strong nonminimal local controls. Compare against author nonminimal refinement and moment-profiled proper Procrustes, not only minimal generators or explicit pair enumeration. The two-cone construction is an elementary relaxation of lifted monomials; merely applying it is not yet an identifiable research contribution. Poor recovery, collapse, loose gaps, or no benefit over the same-cost controls would close this implementation hypothesis without a paper claim.

Search families covered quoted/unquoted combinations of affine monocular depth, nonminimal calibration, interpoint/pairwise distances, convex/semidefinite relaxation, variable projection, and pose/shape alignment, including current 2026 results. Search misses, unindexed work, and source differences remain possible. The statement here is only “no exact duplicate found in this bounded screen,” not “first” or “novel.”
