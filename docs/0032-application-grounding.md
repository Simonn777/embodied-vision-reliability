# 0032 application grounding — source-only, 2026-10-20

**Conclusion.** The defensible application link is pixel-accurate visible-object support for image-plane tracking from a moving camera. The present experiment evaluates a known crop refinement baseline on permanently exposed, single-target DAVIS development recordings. It does not yet evaluate driving, multi-object association, ego-motion estimation, or embodied action. An improvement here would justify a transfer question, not answer it.

## Two primary works read

1. **Voigtlaender et al., “MOTS: Multi-Object Tracking and Segmentation,” CVPR 2019.** [Official publication](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Voigtlaender_MOTS_Multi-Object_Tracking_and_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.html); [author manuscript read](https://arxiv.org/html/1902.03604v2). Read §§1–2, §3 dataset construction, §4 Eqs. (1)–(6), §5 mask propagation/tracking including Eq. (9), and §6 setup/online caveat. KITTI MOTS supplies a direct moving-camera driving relation: track cars and pedestrians with instance masks and persistent identities. Its sMOTSA combines matched mask IoU, false positives, and identity switches, normalized by ground-truth instance count. The mask-propagation baseline associates instances using optical-flow-warped mask overlap. This is a concrete route by which segmentation can affect tracking. The paper also distinguishes an online ConvLSTM alternative from its two-frame-lookahead 3D-convolution variant; a causal comparison cannot silently borrow that future access.

2. **Kim et al., “Video Panoptic Segmentation,” CVPR 2020.** [Official publication](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/html/Kim_Video_Panoptic_Segmentation_CVPR_2020_paper.html); [author manuscript read](https://arxiv.org/html/2006.11339v1). Read §3 task and VPQ Eq. (1), §4 Cityscapes-VPS, and §§5.1–5.2 architecture/inference. Cityscapes-VPS evaluates driving videos with semantic classes and temporally consistent instance tubes. VPQ uses tube overlap, matched tubes, and false-positive/false-negative tubes; longer evaluation windows expose temporal inconsistency that image-only segmentation scores miss. The method combines motion-aligned feature fusion with instance association; its inference processes frames sequentially. Thus motion-aware refinement and tracking are already established controls, not a novel application claim.

Access was limited to these two works through web source-page text. Both official CVF PDF requests returned 403, so the detailed method reads used the linked author HTML versions. No repository, dataset, annotations, weights, or scientific arrays were fetched. No current-state-of-the-art claim or benchmark split-count claim is made from these reads.

## What would be measurable

The narrow transferable hypothesis is: **at fixed detections and track identities, a better emitted mask improves the visible spatial support assigned to each tracked object.** That can be tested using matched-mask IoU/MOTSP and the segmentation contribution to sMOTSA, while also reporting FP, FN, and identity switches. It is more specific than claiming general autonomous-driving improvement. Boundary F alone is not a physical clearance, distance, collision-risk, or planning metric.

A separate, stronger association hypothesis would require feeding the refined masks into an unchanged, causal association algorithm, such as flow-warped mask-overlap association, and measuring identity outcomes as well as masks. Same detections, frame access, association rules, and complete crop/flow/encoding cost would be necessary controls. That is a possible future task definition, **not an admitted experiment or additional acquisition**.

For current 0032, native history and pointers remain unchanged. Accordingly, improved output masks cannot by themselves demonstrate improved internal tracking state or later identity recovery. Any observed gain would be attributable to the evaluated output-refinement procedure at its measured extra compute; a same-budget known refinement baseline remains necessary before an algorithmic advantage claim.

## Strongest limitation of DAVIS grounding

The central limitation is the **task and information contract**, beyond cohort size. This pilot starts with a supplied target annotation and evaluates one chosen object ID. Driving MOTS/VPS must handle multiple objects, unknown births and deaths, object association, and—in VPS—semantic classes and stuff regions. The current six generic development recordings do not establish coverage of vehicle-camera appearance, traffic occlusion, weather, road geometry, or calibrated ego motion. Camera motion in a video is not itself a test of ego-motion robustness.

Higher DAVIS target-object J/F therefore cannot establish automatic driving-object discovery, lower identity-switch rates, 3D motion accuracy, or safer action. Nor can results on repeatedly exposed development recordings establish generalization. The accurate present label is **“single-target mask-detail refinement on exposed DAVIS development data.”** Embodied/driving relevance remains a motivated downstream hypothesis requiring its own task, input contract, controls, and untouched evaluation.
