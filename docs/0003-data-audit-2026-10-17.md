## What is frozen now

The protocol fixes a 64-frame, at-most-two-query budget per whole family; audited
or unknown families are quarantined. For untouched proven families, deterministic
seed/hash order allocates 2 additional development / 10 calibration / 20 evaluation
families. There are currently **zero established independent units and no actual
allocation**. Different file hashes or 159 published videos cannot satisfy the
20-independent-family requirement. Exact sample/source identities are acquisition
leads requiring later whole-byte checks, never silently treated as acquired.

The signed same-time identity is r_pred=r_GT_image+r_error; norms do not add.
Here r_error is 2D error curvature, not 3D localization drift. GT projection and
world motion are separate diagnostics. Perspective camera/world contributions
are not declared additive, and m/s² is prohibited without verified units/FPS.
Invalid GT anchors make only the diagnostic undefined; no replacement anchors or
change to the primary visible denominator. Prediction history remains raw/causal,
including rejected/occluded/invalid-logit coordinates, without GT inputs.

BootsTAPIR source/checkpoint identities and the reference residual scale 8 px,
weight 1, cap 10 are unchanged. No policy is selected/tuned here. The new
prospective pilot continuation goal is C=R_aug-0.8*R_native with paired cluster
95% CI upper <0; report absolute risks/delta and coverage too. It is a **planning
judgment**, not power or efficacy evidence. Zero baseline cannot pass. Fewer than
20 independent test families, 5 native-error-bearing families, 20 native matched
errors, sufficient GT support, reachable 80% coverage or 95% defined bootstrap
draws yields INCONCLUSIVE. No adaptive difficult-data additions are allowed.
Mechanism descriptions and synthetic injection controls are distinct from that
continuation decision and cannot substitute for independent reliability evidence.

The [stdlib implementation](../src/evr/motion_design.py) contains geometry,
signed decomposition, raw-history residuals, family allocation, conservative gates
and a canonical commit/blob/index/current/published-ref freeze validator. It
contains **no dataset adapter, model, scoring experiment or evaluate command**.
Hand-computed fixtures cover genuine motion, constant offset, linear/quadratic
error, cancellation, orthogonal components, irregular timestamps, camera motion,
invalid anchors and adversarial freeze provenance. These verify algebra/software,
not formal scientific effects or real tracker behavior. Full standard CI tests
and the existing toy smoke remain publication gates, not new model experiments.
