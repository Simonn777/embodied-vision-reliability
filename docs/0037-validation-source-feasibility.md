## Strong comparisons and causality

[FluxGraph, §§3.2–3.3 and Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19743v1) is a mandatory close transformation comparator: disagreement-triggered proposals and reverse verification already exploit causal history. Its table reports the best VOST J among the compared methods; TubeletGraph is slightly higher on M³-VOS. This is not proof of a universal current leader. Reverse-to-past is not inherently noncausal, but the released pipeline's ordering and any retroactive changes need checking before calling its published result strictly online. SAMURAI is the strongest standard forward tracker in that table on VOST and M³-VOS; SAM2.1 and DAM4SAM also belong in the comparison shortlist. Paper GPU timings cannot establish feasibility on our CPU.

[SAM-PT, §3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.01197v2) already uses positive/negative initial-mask points, visibility filtering and mask refinement; the paper also evaluates optical-flow alternatives. Its PIPS/CoTracker configuration may use temporal windows, so a claimed causal comparator must disclose available lookahead. [VSCOS's author XMem-SC implementation](https://github.com/venom12138/VSCOS) is a further motion-aware prior. D37's inexpensive DIS propagation should face the same-feature ordinary classifier and propagation controls as well as a trustworthy strong tracker. Budget-infeasible baselines cannot be silently replaced and then described as beaten.

## Proposed amendment, not an adopted gate

The charter permits a new task-specific prospective gate; the standing **+3 official J&F on each of two independent held-out sources** remains unchanged today. A defensible transformation-task amendment would be:

1. Freeze a causal first-mask, all-initial-object transformation task, with **untouched VOST plus independently sourced M³-VOS**, only after original recording/URL grouping and nonoverlap are established. Preserve all existing development and protected-final exclusions. One same locked method/configuration must transfer; no source-specific threshold fitting.
2. Require **at least +3 absolute J points AND +3 Jtr points on each source**, against the strongest prespecified eligible causal comparator for each metric/source. Do not exchange a loss on one source or metric for a gain elsewhere. Report M³ Jcc as an additional fragmentation-sensitive check, with its role fixed before access.
3. Require positive paired uncertainty intervals clustered by original recording, complete failure-inclusive coverage, declared multiobject composition, task-matched inputs and full-pipeline cost limits. A small convenience sample cannot support broad benchmark claims; cohort size and inference precision must be justified prospectively, not reduced until a result becomes positive.
4. Freeze all metric versions, sampling rules, comparison identities, compute limits and source-selection policy **before final outcomes**. A D37 development result—positive or negative—cannot itself satisfy or rewrite this gate.

Requiring both J and Jtr to improve by three points is deliberately substantial, but it is a *different scientific endpoint*, not a mathematical equivalence to +3 J&F. It requires an explicit charter amendment; HD-EPIC J&F is not an interchangeable rescue endpoint.
