Zero-baseline error can use the multiplicative inequality directly without dividing by zero. Keep these choices in frozen config/source rather than deciding after results.

## Mathematical assessment

The central construction is sound in its stated oracle Gaussian domain. Full input-vector cross-covariance, not merely scalar pair covariance, is the correct requirement. A rowwise 49D MLP with a literal constant-zero center is mathematically equivalent to the 48D prototype. No BatchNorm across samples, global normalization, raw-center skip, spatial feature convolution, estimated-C side input or other target-dependent feature is permitted by that guarantee. R2R must use the SAME full 49D eta draw in both its input and target; the protocol does so. Independent augmentation per patch is sufficient for this pointwise baseline; a globally coherent augmentation field is unnecessary here.

The final fitted predictor is `f_theta(y)(z_i)`: theta depends on the same whole noisy image and can reuse target noise through training at other centers. Therefore the population risk identity for a fixed predictor is **not** an unbiased-risk/generalization guarantee for the resulting single-image fitted network. The existing finite-training caveat is appropriate; make that dependency explicit in result wording and do not call final predictions independent of n_i. The screen can empirically test this restricted transductive procedure against its held-out reference values, but cannot repair the theorem by citing fixed updates or low capacity.
