## Four-fold fitting and prediction

The eleven output arms in fixed order are native, logit, absolute, prototype, nn, ordinary_relations, bilateral, signed_no_gram, signed_gram, displaced_gram, rgb256. Ten learned arms times four held parents yield40 fits, all in one complete bounded fit-and-prediction operation. They are pointwise affine readouts with fixed nonlinear outputs; no convolutional head from0033 is retrained.

Take min(Q,1024) training sites per noninitial frame by the exact fixed uniform row-major rank rule, including endpoints. Coordinates are selected before labels. Only those binary target labels enter the objective; 255 is background, as in the inherited metric convention. A frame without eligible sites contributes zero data loss and remains in its parent's denominator. Each fitting sample weight is1/(3*(parent_noninitial_frame_count)*frame_sample_count). Do not renormalize these weights. Floating-point summation tolerances validate arithmetic only, not scientific thresholds.

The caller loads the four parents' sampled training labels once for cross-fitting. For a particular held-parent fold it constructs fitting arrays only from the other three parents. Its labels/statistics never enter its own predictor or optimizer. This is explicit computational cross-fitting; it is not a false claim that no label from that parent was used for the different folds. All hyperparameters and source remain fixed before this access, and no held-parent metrics are computed inside fitting/prediction.

For the absolute arm, only its last32 native-feature columns are normalized. Mean and population std are computed in float64 over the fixed sampled sites of the three fitting parents, std floor1e−6; RGB and other natural-scale quantities are not standardized. These statistics weight sampled sites equally, while the objective separately balances parents/frames. The held parent's feature distribution is never used to fit normalization.

Ordinary outputs are native_logit+4*tanh(Xw), initialized w=0. Signed outputs are native_logit+4*e*sigmoid(Xtheta), initialized theta=0 except intercept−4; their initial corrections are explicitly nonzero. A zero-evidence signed query is exactly unchanged, and its correction cannot reverse the signed evidence. This may be harmful if pseudo-label color evidence is wrong. There is no same-initial-output claim between these two families, nor an equal-effective-capacity claim from different feature dimensions.
