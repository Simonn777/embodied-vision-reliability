# Change the development question after 0034

2026-10-20. Source and metadata preparation only. No new scientific payload, model call, prediction, score, training or budget hold. The research goal remains active and unachieved; no manuscript.

The 0034 experiment closes its fixed RGB-relation recipe: native J&F95.4060107%, signedGram95.3503264%, every learned arm below native. Earlier0030–33 exact variants also remain closed. Four TRAIN recordings and repeated use of two DEV recordings are narrow development evidence. Native F97.1823% leaves little boundary-score headroom; it does not prove all refinement impossible or identify a unique failure cause. A three-point J&F gain would consume about65% of this population's remaining arithmetic deficit. More tiny corrections on this same population are not the next step.

The next question being prepared is **omission of whole target descendants after a physical transformation**. It concerns tracking the material originating in an initial object when it is cut, torn or unfolded. It is distinct from repairing a contour around an already localized target. This supplies a concrete embodied-perception link, not a robot-control result. Model architecture does not impose connected masks; the suspected failure concerns learned predictions and their selection.

## Primary prior constraints

[TubeletGraph, NeurIPS2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04678v1) already discovers additional entity tracks and filters them using spatial and semantic consistency. [FluxGraph, July2026 preprint v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19743v1) already triggers candidate discovery from SAM2 mask disagreement and filters candidates with historical reverse overlap. Reverse traversal can use only already observed frames; merely calling it causal would not distinguish a method. Sparse fragment birth, reverse ancestry and merging tracks are therefore strong prior controls, not inventions here. Neither full system has been reproduced locally.

[VOST, CVPR2023, §§3.2–3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.06200v2#S3.SS3) assigns descendants the initial object's identity, ignores ambiguous regions, and reports J plus last-quarter Jtr. The authors deliberately omit F and state kitchen/subject separation across official splits. This does not provide a local within-TRAIN recording map. VOST JF is not an official endpoint, and an extra computed F cannot silently meet the existing official-JF manuscript gate.

A proposed change from reverse-IoU to directed containment is also insufficient novelty. For a true reverse fragment B contained in parent P, IoU=|B|/|P| can be small despite perfect containment. That is a conditional geometric fact, not evidence it occurs frequently. Candidate-normalized coverage is already used in TubeletGraph. If a negative score is merely the complement of parent containment, a log-likelihood transform adds no information. Independent identity/visibility evidence would need to beat an ordinary same-evidence classifier before supporting a contribution.

## The one necessary empirical question

Do the already computed alternatives of qualified SAM2.1 tiny contain omitted target material in this different task? Capture the ordinary three-mask bank while leaving its chosen native history unchanged. Compare complete-clip, all-initial-object native predictions and fixed union controls. A privileged support ceiling is only a necessary information check; it is not an achievable gain or an invented selector. If the candidate bank lacks material, stop this particular proposal source before fitting a teacher or building a graph.

This is deliberately a known-baseline question. A later causal verifier would require actual candidate-admission gains against the same proposals and complete cost. Any future-supervised variant must beat the identical verifier trained directly from the same dense training labels, as well as the relevant historical reverse-consistency control. No such verifier is admitted or implemented now.
