2026-10-20. No asset, image, mask, array, model, or experiment was accessed. This note allocates no validation cohort and changes no gate.

**Conclusion:** transformation VOS has plausible official J&F sources, especially VISOR and HD-EPIC, but this bounded check does **not** establish two affordable, independent, untouched confirmatory cohorts. D35's VOST J/Jtr opportunity screen cannot satisfy the unchanged final requirement of at least +3 absolute official J&F points on two independent sources. A VOST-only transformation claim would require a separately approved, prospective task-level gate before confirmatory outcomes; it cannot silently replace or fulfill the current goal.

## Two relevant sources

| Source | Verified contract | Independence / practical status |
|---|---|---|
| **EPIC-KITCHENS VISOR**, NeurIPS Datasets and Benchmarks 2022 | The [author VOS repository](https://github.com/epic-kitchens/VISOR-VOS), README Evaluation and Dataset Structure, publishes J, F and J&F and uses DAVIS evaluation with an explicit change to **include the last frame**. It provides validation and unseen-kitchen split conventions, video-ID-based filenames, and a 480p conversion path. The [official dataset site](https://epic-kitchens.github.io/VISOR/site) supplies frames/annotations and states dataset CC BY-NC 4.0; repository code separately states CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. | Task fits active objects undergoing transformations. However, VISOR derives from EPIC-KITCHENS, which also contributes VOST recordings. Dataset names therefore do not prove independent recordings. A selected-parent mapping against all exposed VOST/DAVIS development sources is necessary. Per-video filenames support a possible small complete cohort, but exact public subset transfer bytes and transport were **not verified**. No budget-fit claim. |
| **HD-EPIC**, CVPR 2025 | [Paper v2 §5.3 and Appendix D.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04144v2) explicitly define mean J&F for a 1,000-sequence long-term VOS benchmark. Each object's **first appearance**, not necessarily video frame zero, initializes memory; only memory and annotated evaluation frames are passed to the tested models. The paper acknowledges this sparse-input limitation. | Newly collected Aria recordings provide a credible separate recording source. Its challenge includes occlusion/location changes, not exclusively fragmentation. [Official site](https://hd-epic.github.io/site/) licenses the data CC BY-NC 4.0 and lists MP4 videos at 115.5 GB, far above available budget. A complete sparse benchmark subset might be small, but exact benchmark manifest, evaluator release, per-recording transfer sizes and extraction route remain unresolved. [Official annotation README](https://github.com/hd-epic/hd-epic-annotations) exposes participant/video IDs and track associations; it is not a verified turnkey VOS evaluator. |

HD-EPIC is a **prospective candidate**, not an admitted second source. Its masks were initialized with SAM2 and manually corrected, according to the official site; this is an annotation-provenance consideration, not proof that the evaluation recordings trained our checkpoint. Its sparse first-appearance protocol would need an explicit adapter and same-input controls; simply running D35's first-frame/all-frame loop would not replicate it.

## Non-candidates and exposure limits

The exact name **TE-TG** did not resolve to a verified independent dataset in the bounded search. Do not count it. [TubeletGraph v1 §4.1 / Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.04678v1) explicitly builds **VOST-TAS from VOST validation**, so that extension is not an independent recording source. Its transformation-description metrics also do not supply an official J&F replacement. The same paper's VSCOS is EPIC-derived; M³-VOS is a possible separate online-video source, but its own release/license/official J&F/bytes were not verified here. Neither is admitted. Generic fragment discovery and reverse ancestry are already covered by TubeletGraph and [FluxGraph v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.19743v1); none of this source inventory establishes a method contribution.

[SAM2 v2 Appendix D.2.2 and F.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00714v2) declares released-model training on SA-V manual, internal video and SA-1B, and treats VISOR/VOST as zero-shot evaluations. This is stronger evidence than inferring exposure from a dataset name, but the internal-video inventory is not fully public. It does not prove duplicate-free selected parents. HD-EPIC's later publication is likewise not a proof of absence from earlier training. Record the qualified checkpoint's published training declaration and the remaining uncertainty; avoid asserting guaranteed uncontaminated evaluation.

## Consequence for the unchanged gate

A route that preserves the present goal is possible **only after** prospectively fixing two official J&F source protocols, independent raw-recording cohorts, complete subset costs, all objects/frames and source-specific initialization/evaluation rules, with untouched labels until final prediction seals. VISOR plus independently recorded HD-EPIC is a candidate route, not a completed feasibility result. Protected DAVIS material remains untouched and unallocated by this note. If these conditions cannot be met and the work instead centers on VOST J/Jtr or transformation-graph scores, the scientific objective and substantial-gain gate must be resolved prospectively before any confirmatory outcome; the old J&F goal remains unmet.
